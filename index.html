TradeBit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the TradeBit mobile app ("TradeBit", "the app", "we") handles information. TradeBit is published by Appinclive (an independent developer, "the developer") and is a planning, journaling and market-news tool for trading. It is not a brokerage or trading platform.

In short: your plans and records stay on your device. We do not operate any server that receives your data, we do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal information. The app does connect to the internet to download public news headlines (see section 4).

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Plans you create: plan name, market type, currency, starting capital, target percentage, duration and start date.
  • Daily or monthly records: dates, your entered percentage results, calculated capital figures, status (profit, loss, break-even, skipped) and notes you type.
  • Optional profile name you choose to enter.
  • App preferences: light or dark theme and calendar settings.

This information is created by you, stays on your device, and is not transmitted to us or anyone else by the app.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or receive: your name or email address (unless you choose to email support), phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, device identifiers, financial account or payment details, or usage analytics.

TradeBit does not connect to your bank, broker or trading account, and it never asks for passwords or card numbers.

4. Permissions and network use

TradeBit does not request any sensitive Android permissions (such as contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage; these are also blocked in the app's configuration). It uses the standard internet permission for one purpose only: the News tab downloads public headlines from news publishers' RSS feeds (for example Economic Times, Moneycontrol, Mint, Business Standard, BusinessLine, CNBC and BBC) and from the public feeds of official bodies (the Reserve Bank of India and the US Federal Reserve).

When the app requests these feeds, the publisher's server can see technical information that any internet request includes, such as your IP address and device/app type, and handles it under its own privacy policy. The app does not send your plans, records, notes, profile name or any other personal data in these requests, and we do not receive them.

The downloaded headlines are saved on your device so the list can be shown without re-downloading. Tapping a headline opens the article in your browser on the publisher's website, which is governed by that publisher's policies.

5. Advertising, analytics and third-party SDKs

TradeBit contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone.

The only ways information leaves the app are actions you start yourself or the news download described above:

  • News tab: the app contacts the news publishers' public feeds to download headlines. No personal data from the app is included.
  • Opening a headline: opens the publisher's website in your browser.
  • Contact support: opens your email app with a pre-filled message to asktradebit@gmail.com. You decide whether to send it.
  • Share app: shares a link to the app's Google Play page.

7. Device backups and Google Play

If automatic backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include app data in a backup stored in your Google account, under Google's terms and privacy policy. We have no access to such backups. You can manage this in your device's Google settings.

Installing the app from Google Play is also subject to Google's own privacy practices, which we do not control.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings > Your data > Delete all data permanently erases every plan, record, note, your profile name, calendar settings and the saved news list.
  • Delete a single plan from the Plans screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it once deleted. The app has no user accounts, so there is no separate account to delete.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private sandbox, which other apps cannot normally access. We do not add extra encryption inside the app, so we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping it up to date.

10. Children's privacy

TradeBit is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or any higher minimum age required in your country).

11. Your rights

Because your data is stored only on your device, you already have full control: you can view it in the app and delete it at any time. If you have questions about your privacy or a request under the laws that apply to you, email us and we will respond. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR or similar laws) you may have rights to access, correct or erase your data; since we hold none, deletion is done by you in the app as described in section 8.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always available in the app at Settings > Legal > Privacy Policy and on our Google Play listing, and the "Last updated" date will change.
