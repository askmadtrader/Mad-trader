1. Introduction

Mad Trader ("the App", "we", "our") is a personal trading compounding planner and journal developed for individual use. This Privacy Policy describes exactly what data is collected, by whom, and how it is used. By using Mad Trader, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. What data Mad Trader collects

Mad Trader itself does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal or financial data to our own servers. We have no backend servers.

The App integrates third-party SDKs — Firebase Crashlytics, PostHog Analytics, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing — that collect limited technical data as described in Sections 4 and 5. This is standard for a free app with an optional paid tier, and is disclosed in full below.

3. Your trading data — local storage

All data you enter into Mad Trader — capital amounts, P&L, dates, notes, journal entries, and trading plans — is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud backup (Section 6). It is never accessible to us or any third party. It can be exported as a JSON file at any time, and permanently deleted via Settings → Reset All Data.

Photo library (optional): If you attach a chart screenshot to a journal entry, the selected image is compressed and copied into the App's private local storage only — it is never uploaded anywhere unless you separately enable Google Drive backup. Photo access is requested only at the moment you tap "Add Photo," never automatically, and you can deny it and still use every other feature of the App.

4. Third-party services & SDKs

The App uses the following third-party services. Each collects data independently under their own privacy policies, linked below.

Google AdMob (Free tier only)
Active in: free tier Collects device data for ads

Free-tier users see banner ads. AdMob collects your advertising ID, device model, OS version, screen size, and IP address (used to infer general location) for ad targeting. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Opt out of personalised ads via your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Pro subscribers see no ads. AdMob is not active for Pro users.

Firebase Crashlytics (All users)
Active in: all tiers Collects crash and stability data

Collects anonymous crash logs and stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, a randomly generated installation UUID, and the crash timestamp — to help us fix bugs. Does NOT collect your trading data, journal entries, or any personal identifiers. Governed by Firebase's Privacy Policy.

PostHog Analytics (All users)
Active in: all tiers Collects anonymous usage events

Collects lightweight, anonymous usage events (e.g. "plan created," "journal entry added," "app opened") tied to a random device ID generated and stored locally on your device — not your name, email, or trading figures. No prices, P&L, or notes are ever sent. Used only to understand which features are used so we can improve the App. Governed by PostHog's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing (Pro subscribers only)
Active in: Pro purchase flow

Processes your Mad Trader Pro subscription payment. We never see or store your card, UPI, or other payment details — that is handled entirely by Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a subscription is active, via a purchase token stored locally on your device. See Section 5 for full subscription details.

Google Sign-In & Google Drive (Optional — user-initiated only)
Active in: opt-in only

See Section 6 for full details. This is only active if you choose to sign in with Google to enable cloud backup. Nothing is sent to Google Drive by default.

5. Mad Trader Pro subscription

Mad Trader Pro (₹599/year) unlocks unlimited plans, unlimited journal entries, cloud backup, PDF export, Excel export, and an ad-free experience. Analytics & charts are free for all users regardless of tier.

  • Billing: Handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Mad Trader has no backend server and never processes payments directly.
  • Auto-renewal: The subscription automatically renews each year at ₹599 unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date, per standard Google Play subscription terms.
  • Cancel anytime: Go to Settings → Manage Subscription, or directly via the Google Play Store app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds: Governed entirely by Google Play's refund policy. We do not process refunds directly — please contact Google Play support or use Google Play's own refund request flow.
  • Restoring a purchase: If you reinstall the App or switch devices, sign in to Play Store with the same Google account that purchased Pro — your subscription is detected and restored automatically. A manual "Restore Purchases" option is also available on the Upgrade screen.

6. Optional cloud backup (Google Drive)

Cloud backup via Google Drive is entirely opt-in. It is not enabled by default and requires you to sign in with Google in Settings → Cloud Backup.

If you sign in with Google:

  • Google Sign-In shares your account's name, email address, and profile photo with the App, so we can show which account is connected
  • Your data is stored in a private AppData folder in your Google Drive — hidden from your regular Drive view, so only Mad Trader can read or write to it
  • The App only requests the narrow drive.appdata permission scope — it cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive
  • Data is transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to Google's servers; we do not operate any backup server ourselves
  • The backup is automatically deleted if you uninstall the app
  • You can restore your data or sign out at any time from Settings

If you never sign in with Google, no account data or backup is ever created or transmitted.

7. Permissions

Mad Trader explicitly blocks camera, location, contacts, and microphone access. The following permissions are requested only when needed:

  • Storage / Files: To export and import local backup JSON files
  • Photos: Only if you choose to attach a chart screenshot to a journal entry
  • Notifications: Only if you turn on reminders in Settings → Notifications. Off by default. All scheduling happens locally on your device — no data is sent anywhere to deliver these reminders.
  • Internet: Required by Crashlytics (crash reports), PostHog (analytics events), optional Google Drive backup, AdMob (Free tier ads), and Google Play Billing (Pro subscription)

No other permissions are used.

9. NSE holiday data

The App contains pre-loaded NSE (National Stock Exchange of India) holidays for 2025–2030. This data is embedded in the App and does not require an internet connection. Always verify the official NSE calendar before trading.

10. Children's privacy

Mad Trader is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App deals with real financial planning and trading concepts, it is intended for users aged 18 and above.

11. Your rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: All data is visible within the App at all times
  • Export: Export everything as a JSON file via Settings → Backup
  • Delete: Permanently delete all local data via Settings → Reset All Data
  • Opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • Cloud backup removal: Sign out of Google from Settings → Cloud Backup
  • Manage or cancel Pro subscription: Settings → Manage Subscription
